Common Questions

Before You Reach Out,
Here Are the Answers.

Do you work with clients outside Kenya?
Absolutely. I work with clients across Africa, the UK, the US, and beyond. Everything — writing, web development, AI consulting — is handled fully remotely. Communication via email, WhatsApp, or Zoom.
How do payments work?
For new clients, I require a 50% deposit before starting. The balance is due on delivery. I accept M-Pesa, bank transfer, PayPal, and Wise for international clients.
How long does a typical project take?
Blog articles: 2–4 days. Landing pages: 5–7 days. Full websites: 2–3 weeks. AI integration packages: 1–2 weeks. Rush delivery is available for an additional fee.
What if I'm not happy with the work?
All services include at least one round of revisions. My goal is always your satisfaction. If something's not right, I fix it — straightforward, no drama.
Can you combine services in one package?
Yes, and many clients do exactly this. A website + copy + SEO strategy, or a tutoring plan + academic editing. Get in touch and I'll put together a custom quote for your needs.
Do you sign NDAs or confidentiality agreements?
Yes, for clients who require it — especially for data annotation, ghostwriting, or proprietary content projects. Just let me know and I'll review and sign before we start.
