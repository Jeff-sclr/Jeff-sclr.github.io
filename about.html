My Story

I Didn't Choose One Thing.
I Chose All of Them.

It started with writing. I picked up my first freelance article job five years ago — a 1,000-word blog post for a small business — and something clicked. Words had power. The right ones could make someone stop scrolling, trust a brand, and take action. I wanted to master that.

But mastering words led me to websites. If I'm writing the copy, why can't I build the page it lives on? So I taught myself HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Then React. Then WordPress. Then I started noticing that the businesses I built sites for had the same problem — they didn't understand how to talk to their audience online. So I started teaching them.

Teaching businesses led me to tutoring students. I realised that the ability to explain things clearly — truly clearly, without jargon — was rarer than I expected. So I started tutoring, and students started passing exams they'd been struggling with for years.

Then AI arrived. Not as a replacement for any of this — but as rocket fuel for all of it. I spent months learning prompt engineering, data annotation, and how to integrate AI tools into real workflows. Now I help businesses use it right, and I use it myself to deliver better work, faster.

That's the arc. Five years. Four services. One person who genuinely loves every single one of them. That combination — rare as it is — is exactly what my clients come back for.

The Journey

Five Years.
Built One Skill at a Time.

Every service I offer today started as a problem I chose to solve.

2019 — The Beginning

First Freelance Writing Gig

Picked up my first paid writing job — a blog article for a small Nairobi business. Delivered it on time, got a 5-star review, and realised this was something I could actually build a career on.

2020 — Going Digital

Launched into Web Development

Started teaching myself HTML and CSS after a writing client asked if I could also build their website. Said yes, then figured it out. Built my first three sites that year — each one better than the last.

2021 — Teaching Others

Began Academic Tutoring

A family friend's child was struggling with English and business studies. I offered to help. Within two months their grades improved significantly. Word spread, and tutoring became a proper service.

2022 — Scaling Up

First International Clients

Landed writing and development contracts with clients in the UK and US. Proved that quality work done from Nairobi competes with anyone, anywhere. Grew my portfolio to 50+ completed projects.

2023 — The AI Era

Entered AI Training & Consulting

Started data annotation work for AI companies and deep-dived into prompt engineering. Helped several businesses integrate AI tools into their workflows — cutting hours of manual work every week.

2024–Present

One Expert. Full Stack.

Operating across all four services simultaneously — writing, development, tutoring, and AI — with a growing roster of repeat clients who trust me as their go-to across multiple disciplines.
