Common Questions

Before You Reach Out,
Here Are the Answers.

How quickly can you start on my project? +
For most projects I can begin within 24–48 hours of confirming the scope and receiving an initial deposit. Urgent same-day projects are sometimes possible — just ask.
Do you work with international clients? +
Absolutely. I work with clients across Africa, Europe, North America, and beyond. All communication is online and I accept international payments via M-Pesa, PayPal, and bank transfer.
What's your revision policy? +
Every project includes at least two rounds of revisions. I'd rather spend the extra time getting it right than deliver something you're not proud of.
Can you handle multiple services at once? +
Yes — and it's actually one of my biggest advantages. If you need a website built and the content written for it, or a tutoring session with a follow-up summary document, I handle both without any coordination gaps.
How do I pay? +
I accept M-Pesa, PayPal, Wise, and bank transfers. A 50% deposit is required to start, with the balance due on delivery. Full payment may be required for smaller jobs under $50.
What if I'm not sure what I need? +
That's fine — actually common. Just describe your problem or goal and I'll recommend the right approach. The consultation is free and there's no pressure to proceed if it's not the right fit.
